METRONOME＆JAM In-App Purchase

Metronome Mode

The core feature for playing click sounds in sync with your tempo. Adjust BPM by dragging the number left/right or tapping the +/− buttons. Tap continuously to automatically measure BPM from your tap intervals. Change time signatures, sound kits, and rhythm patterns (eighth notes, triplets, shuffle, etc.) with dedicated buttons. Tap the beat pad (PAD) to toggle accent or mute for each beat. Adjust overall volume with the volume slider, and set a practice timer to automatically stop after your target duration.

Jam (Rhythm Loop) Mode

A drum loop feature featuring 3 parts: hi-hat, snare, and kick. Tap the preset name at the top (Rock, Funk, Shuffle, etc.) to select a genre, or assemble your own custom beat pattern with the 16-step sequencer. Tap each step to cycle volume through 4 levels: OFF → Ghost → Normal → Accent. Adjust individual part volume balance and swing (groove feeling) with sliders, with BPM configurable independently from the metronome.

My List (Preset Storage)

Save up to 100 of your favorite metronome and jam configurations with custom names. Simply tap a saved preset to instantly recall all settings—including BPM, time signature, sound kit, and drum patterns—and automatically switch to the corresponding tab. Reorder, rename, or delete presets seamlessly from the "Edit" button.

Apple Watch Support

  • Play/Stop, change BPM, and switch time signatures or sound kits directly from Apple Watch
  • Haptic vibration feedback enables silent tapping in rhythm with the tempo
  • Keep precise rhythm through wrist vibrations even in quiet environments

Customization

Customize the app's overall appearance and behavior. Choose your preferred appearance mode (Light, Dark, or System), select from 7 color themes, and toggle iPhone vibration, screen flash, or Apple Watch vibration ON/OFF. When using Bluetooth earphones, fine-tune audio and haptic synchronization with the latency compensation slider (0–500ms). Check your premium status or upgrade directly from this screen.

iPad Split View Support

Freely adjust the app size to use it as a convenient mini player alongside sheet music or other music apps.

Pro Version (One-Time Purchase)

Upgrade to Pro to unlock all premium features, including all sound kits, subdivided rhythms, preset storage, and Apple Watch integration. A single one-time purchase grants lifetime access.
