Metronome & Jam Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Metronome & Jam ("the App") handles user information.
Metronome & Jam does not collect any personal information. The App also does not use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar purposes.

1. Apple Watch and Microphone

Metronome & Jam works with Apple Watch to provide features such as haptic feedback in time with the tempo and control of the App from Apple Watch. The App also requests access to the microphone to follow the tempo of your performance.
Information exchanged with Apple Watch and audio signals captured by the microphone are used solely to provide the App's features and are processed on your device. Audio signals and recordings are not stored or retained, and are never transmitted to external servers or shared with third parties.

2. In-App Purchases

Metronome & Jam offers in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information, such as credit card details.
For more information about how Apple handles purchase-related information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store settings such as your preferred tempo and other app preferences locally on your device to provide its features.
This information is used only for the operation of the App and is not transmitted to the developer.

4. Disclaimer

Metronome & Jam is provided to support tempo and rhythm practice and musical performance.
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not liable for any direct or indirect damages arising from the use of the App.
The App may also be affected by changes to Apple Watch, iPhone, or operating system specifications, network conditions, or the condition of your device. As a result, some features may not function as expected in certain circumstances.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as necessary.
If significant changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page. Changes will take effect when the updated Privacy Policy is posted.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Metronome & Jam, please contact us at:
