MovinRings Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MovinRings ("the App") handles user information and health data.
MovinRings respects your privacy and does not collect or transmit personal information or health data without your permission. The App also does not use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar purposes.

1. HealthKit Data

MovinRings uses Apple's HealthKit to access activity data, such as walking, running, and cycling distances, with your permission. This data is used solely to display your activity distance and progress toward your goals through rings, charts, and other features.
HealthKit data is processed on your device and is never transmitted to external servers or shared or sold to third parties.

2. Apple Watch Integration

MovinRings works with Apple Watch to provide activity information and other Apple Watch features. Information exchanged between Apple Watch and iPhone is used solely to provide the App's features and is not transmitted to the developer.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store settings and other preferences locally on your device to provide its features. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

4. Disclaimer

MovinRings is provided as a tool to help you track and manage your activity and exercise.
The information displayed by the App is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or any other medical purpose.
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not liable for any direct or indirect damages arising from the use of the App.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as necessary. If significant changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page. Changes will take effect when the updated Privacy Policy is posted.
