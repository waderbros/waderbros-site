MovinRings Help

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Can I use it on Apple Watch alone?
A. Yes. It operates standalone on Apple Watch, allowing you to configure and check target distances, units, and ring colors.

Q. Do iPhone and Apple Watch sync?
A. Yes. Data and settings automatically sync. Changes made on either device are reflected on the other immediately.

Q. How is "Walking + Running" data handled?
A. "Walking + Running" distance is the combined total estimated distance detected by iPhone and Apple Watch throughout the day. It is managed separately from individually recorded workouts.

Q. Where is data stored?
A. All data is securely stored on your device via Apple HealthKit and is never transmitted to developers or third parties.

Q. How do I change the app language?
A. App and widget display languages depend on your iPhone language settings. You can change it via:
Settings → MovinRings → Preferred Language (Supports English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Dutch, Chinese, Korean, Thai).


🛠️ Troubleshooting

If widget language does not switch

  1. Check iPhone language settings in Settings → General → Language & Region.
  2. Check MovinRings app language in Settings → MovinRings → Preferred Language.
  3. Restart both iPhone and Apple Watch if changes are not reflected.

System Requirements

  • Apple Watch: watchOS 10.6 or later
  • iPhone: iOS 17.6 or later
  • iPad: iPadOS 17.6 or later
